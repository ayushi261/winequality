How it was built

Systematic methodology followed in the Python execution pipeline.

01

Clean & Explore

Loaded winequality.csv (1,599 rows), verified 0 null values, generated correlation heatmap across all 11 physicochemical features.

02

Engineer & Scale

Binarized quality score into Good (≥7) vs Normal (<7). Applied StandardScaler normalization for distance-sensitive algorithms.

03

Train & Optimize

Evaluated Logistic Regression, KNN, and Decision Trees. Performed 5-fold CV GridSearch on Decision Tree to achieve 90.0% accuracy.
